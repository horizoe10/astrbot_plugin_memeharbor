# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-10

First independently branded MemeHarbor release.

- QQ Official-first capture, quoted images and two-message collection.
- Multi-tag search, stable IDs and numbered previews with per-user paging.
- Content deduplication, per-group recent-send selection and cooldowns.
- Tag editing, merging and recoverable deletion.
- Authenticated Plugin Pages gallery with upload, export and import.
- Optional model-based descriptions, OCR, tag suggestions and metadata search.
- Slash/bare-command compatibility and duplicate-handler protection.
- Independent identity, non-destructive legacy copying and settings import.
- Catalog compatibility with the earlier local Image Replay 2.x enhancement line.
